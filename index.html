MealPebble privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Android and iPhone

MealPebble has no account, no ads and no tracking. We do not receive your diary. On Android it is left out of your phone's backup to Google; an iPhone may include it in your iCloud backup. Files you share go where you choose.

Who we are

MealPebble is made by Prasanna Muthukumaran, India. In this policy, "we" means the maker of MealPebble. You can reach us at mealpebble@gmail.com.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything you enter is saved in a database on your own phone:

  • Meals and drinks: the dishes, how much, the time, where you ate (home, office, hotel, street or ordered in), the food type you chose, and the cost if you add one.
  • Your usual dishes and places, reminders, weekly goals, city and theme.
  • Dishes you add yourself.
  • If you turn on the health log: the medicines you log, when you took them, the reason if you add one, the times of any regular tablets, and how you felt when you log it (a headache or acidity, say, how bad, and when), with or without a tablet. Unless you switch it off, it also keeps your toilet visits: when, and loose, hard or normal if you mark it.
  • If you turn on cigarettes or drinks (each is its own choice, off unless you turn it on): each cigarette, beedi or vape you log and when, and each drink with its type, size, how many, when, and the cost if you add one.

We don't run a server for the app, and the app makes no internet connections, so none of this reaches us. On Android the app does not even have permission to use the internet, so the phone itself would stop any connection.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect personal information, usage analytics, crash reports, advertising IDs or location. There are no third-party analytics or advertising tools in the app.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: for the reminders you choose. They are scheduled on your phone, and nothing is sent to us. Reminders for regular tablets never name the medicine, and no notification mentions cigarettes or drinks.
  • Run after restart: so your reminders come back after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for small taps of feedback when you log something.
  • Fingerprint or face: only if you turn on app lock, to ask for your fingerprint, face or phone PIN when the app opens. Your phone checks it; the app only learns whether it matched, and never sees or keeps your fingerprint or face.

The app doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos. While app lock is on, the app shows blank in your recent apps and screenshots of it are blocked.

Backups and copies

  • Phone backup: on Android, MealPebble is left out of your phone's backup to your Google account, so your diary is not copied there. Some phones can still copy an app's data straight to a new phone when you set it up. On an iPhone, if iCloud backup is on, it may include your diary, and your saved health log, cigarettes and drinks even if you later turn them off. That backup is run by Apple under its own privacy policy, and we can't see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.
  • "Save a copy": creates an unencrypted file of your whole diary, including any saved health log, cigarettes and drinks, even when they are turned off, and opens your phone's share menu. Share it only with people or services you trust. The file goes only where you send it, such as your own Google Drive.
  • "Bring in a saved diary": reads only the file you pick.

Things you share

When you share your day card or a doctor report, the app makes an image or a PDF on your phone and opens the share menu. It goes only to the app or person you choose. We never receive it. Cigarettes and drinks are never on the day card or in the doctor report.

The health log

Medicine information is sensitive, so the health log is off unless you turn it on, it stays on your phone like the rest of your diary, and medicine names are never shown in notification text. MealPebble is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Cigarettes and drinks

Cigarettes and drinks are two separate choices, each off unless you turn it on in first run or Settings. They sit on the back of Today, out of sight until you turn the page over, and stay on your phone like the rest of your diary. The app keeps counts, times and costs only: it gives no advice, warnings or scores, and nothing is sold or promoted. Turning either off hides it and keeps what you logged; Settings › Delete everything removes it.

Your choices

  • Settings › Delete everything removes your diary from the app database. Previously exported files and reports are separate copies.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone.
  • Copies you saved yourself, phone backups and temporary export files may remain until you or the system delete them. We cannot erase copies held by another app or service.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You can still write to us with any question about your data or this policy, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

MealPebble is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that sends information off your phone, such as optional crash reports or a paid backup service, we will update this policy before that version is released, change the date above, and explain it in the app before it is turned on.
